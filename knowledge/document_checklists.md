# Loan Intake Document Checklists

## Personal loan, salaried
Identity proof, address proof, income proof (latest salary slips), and a bank statement covering the last 6 months.

## Personal loan, self-employed
Identity proof, address proof, a bank statement covering the last 6 months, income tax returns for the last 2 years, and business proof (GST registration or shop establishment certificate).

## Two-wheeler loan
Identity proof and address proof. Income proof is required only when the loan amount is above 1,50,000.

## Home loan
Identity proof, address proof, income proof, a 6-month bank statement, income tax returns for 2 years, and property documents (sale agreement or allotment letter).

## High-value applications
For any loan above 10,00,000, income tax returns for the last 2 years are required, whatever the product.

## Document freshness
A bank statement must end within the last 90 days. Salary slips must be from the last 90 days. An expired identity document is treated as missing.

## Cross-document consistency
The applicant name on identity documents must match the application name (case and spacing ignored).
Stated monthly income should be within 20% of the income on the salary slip. A larger gap is a field issue for the underwriter to see.

## Routing bands
Confidence of 0.85 or above goes straight to the underwriter. 0.55 to 0.85 is flagged for review with the specific concerns named. Below 0.55 is returned to the applicant with an exact list of what to fix.
The underwriter still reviews every application, so thresholds can move in small steps as underwriter feedback arrives.

## Resubmissions
When an applicant resubmits, only ask for what is still missing, and acknowledge what was fixed.
